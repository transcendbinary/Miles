# Miles is our Support Chatbot

it isnt replacing our normal direct contact methods. you will ALWAYS be able to email/message us directly and get a real, human response. this is just an additional option for those who prefer it. 

Miles can answer questions about our legal pages, shipping/refund policies, Any products you might be interested in, etc. We have designed him to be transparent and consise and will direct you to where you need to go if he cant answer your questions himself. He does *not* have access to your personal details or order history, Nor does he have access to our backend. He cannot perform actions on your (or our) Behalf. He simply exists to answer questions and make it easier to find information and direct queries.

Miles will ALWAYS remain a seperate window and you will never see a Pop-Up chat window on our website.
